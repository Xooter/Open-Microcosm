# Prepared plates and tissues

Besides the water drop, Open Microcosm offers plate types like a real laboratory (`game/prepared`; data in
`media/`, `vessels/` and `plates/`, see [plates](../content/plates.md)).

## Cell culture

DMEM in an incubator at 37 °C and 10 % CO₂: a circular field of the bottom of a vessel, with the
medium depth of that vessel (volume / area: it sets O₂ supply and CO₂ loss), sterile. Cells are
seeded by density (`[[plate.seeding]]`). Any being in the catalogue can be inoculated into any
plate (Catalogue > Inoculate…, intervention `Inoculate`, reproducible): mixtures are allowed. See
[animal cells](animal-cells.md).

## Slides (`PlateKind::Slide`)

Onion epidermis, *Elodea* leaf, potato with Lugol, root tip with mitoses, leaf epidermis with
stomata, buccal mucosa, Giemsa blood smear, diluted blood and fresh semen.

### Plant tissues

A tissue (`[plate.tissue]`) is a set of **cellulose walls** generated by an arrangement (`rows`,
`polygonal`, `files`, `jigsaw`) and a **living protoplast** (soft body, component `Protoplast`) in
each cell, enclosed by its wall (`confineProtoplasts`; protoplasts do not collide with each other),
with the granules in the cortical layer. Fixed tissues (squashed root tip, potato with Lugol, leaf
epidermis) are walls with their content (`starch`, `mitosis`, `stomata`), not simulated.

### Osmosis

Water crosses the membrane towards where there are more solutes. A cell's volume follows the
medium's osmolarity by **Boyle–van 't Hoff**:

V = Vb + (V0 − Vb) · π0 / π

with V0 the volume in the isotonic medium (osmolarity π0) and Vb the osmotically inactive volume
(`inactive`). It is physics (water through aquaporins), so it runs **in real time**, approaching
equilibrium with a time set by the membrane's hydraulic conductivity (seconds in an erythrocyte,
minutes in a plant cell: `equilibration_s`). The medium's osmolarity comes from salt (as NaCl, 2
ions, osmotic coefficient ~0.93) plus `MediumParams::osmolytes` (sucrose, mannitol): ~16 mOsm in fresh
water, ~340 in DMEM, ~1100 in the sea.

- **Plasmolysis:** in a medium more concentrated than its cell sap (`sap_osmolarity`) the
  protoplast loses water and detaches from the wall; it recovers in water (deplasmolysis).
- **Erythrocytes** (`bio/erythrocytes`): thousands per field, as shaped particles, not soft bodies.
  The human erythrocyte is a biconcave disc of ~7.5–8 µm and ~90 fL, without a nucleus, ~40 %
  osmotically inactive. Hypertonic: it crenates (echinocyte); hypotonic: it swells towards a sphere
  and, past its critical volume (~1.70 ± 0.10 times normal, around 0.42 % NaCl), it bursts
  (**haemolysis**, leaving a ghost). This gives the osmotic fragility curve of normal blood: onset at
  ~0.50 % NaCl, half at ~0.42 %, almost all at ~0.35 % (Parpart et al. 1947, J. Clin. Invest.
  26: 636). Platelets are ~2–3 µm megakaryocyte fragments.

### Fixed preparations

With `fixed`, the preparation does not move and the smear flattens the leukocytes against the glass
(`World::flattenSmear`). Cells that do not move on their own use `locomotion = "none"`. A stain can
be applied on creation (`stain`).

## Limitations

- Stomata do not open or close (roadmap F).
- No cyclosis dependence on temperature in *Elodea*.
- Erythrocytes are not nucleated in any species (no bird or amphibian blood).
